4. Reconciliation logic

Sign convention. Every gap has a bridge_effect, its contribution to (platform total minus bank total).

Order of steps

Load the CSVs with Decimal. The platform date is txn_timestamp[:10] (assert the offset is +05:30). The period and the bank window end (3 Nov) are config constants.
Duplicates first, in both datasets. A repeated txn_id on the platform side, or reference on the bank side, makes every extra row a DUPLICATE gap. Keep the first row for matching. The platform effect is +amount and the bank effect is -amount. The totals still use all rows.
Match the deduped platform rows to the deduped bank rows on txn_id = reference.
Classify each October platform row:
Exact match: the amounts are equal and the bank date is in October.
Sub-paisa rounding difference: the amounts differ by a nonzero amount of 0.005 or less. The effect is the difference.
Amount mismatch: the difference is larger than that. It is unexplained.
Timing: the bank row is dated in November (within the window). The effect is the full platform amount.
No bank row anywhere: unexplained, “missing in bank”.
Unmatched October bank rows. If the row is a refund whose original_reference is not a platform SALE, it is an ORPHAN_REFUND with effect -bank amount. Anything else is unexplained.
Refund lookup for every refund on both sides. Check original_txn_id or original_reference against the platform SALE ids. A broken link is flagged as an orphan, and its effect is whatever it contributes to the totals (0 if it appears on both sides).
Bridge.
Compute the totals independently from the rows: platform October total minus bank October total.
Explained = timing + rounding + duplicate + orphan.
Remainder = difference minus explained.
A separate integrity check compares the remainder with the sum of the unexplained rows’ effects. If they differ, show a “bridge integrity error”. This is what stops the bridge forcing itself to balance.

Each gap gets one plain-language reason, for example: “Platform recorded this on 31 Oct; the bank settled it on 2 Nov, so it falls in November’s statement.” The reconciler never opens ground_truth.csv or expected_totals.json.
